System Features

High Performance. Maximum Precision.

Combining high-speed 360° LiDAR optical distance sensors with custom microcontroller firmware to ensure safe lane changing across all vehicle fleet categories.

🛡️

Safe Lane Changing Assistant

Eliminates dangerous side and rear blind spots during high-speed lane merges. Provides drivers with immediate visual alerts prior to initiating a lane change to protect vulnerable motorcycles and nearby traffic.

🚛

Trailer-First & Universal Compatibility

Purpose-built for heavy commercial articulated trailers where blind spots are most critical, with the added benefit of flexible deployment across buses, delivery vans, and passenger cars.

⚡

Sub-50ms Real-Time Multi-Zone Detection

Scanning LiDAR coverage protects all surrounding zones (Front, Left, Right, Behind) simultaneously. The moment an object enters any detection zone, signal filtering alerts the driver in milliseconds.

🎯

6M - 12M Range Scanning

Calibrated LiDAR range coverage tailored specifically to vehicle lengths, lateral blind spots, and safe merging distances.

Hardware Architecture

System Components & Hardware

At its core, the ASTSS hardware architecture relies on a closely integrated three-part electronic system: a precision LiDAR sensor designed to gather real-time spatial data and distance measurements, a high-performance ESP32 microcontroller serving as the central processing unit to execute logical tasks and manage module communication, and a compact 1.9-inch display screen that renders live telemetry, system status, and visual feedback for the user.
